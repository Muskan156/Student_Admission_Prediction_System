Eligibility Criteria

General Category

Must have passed HSC with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with minimum 45% marks.

Reserved Category

Minimum 40% marks in PCM. Should belong to Maharashtra State reservation category.

Entrance Exam

Must appear for MHT-CET or JEE Main. Admission is based on percentile score.
